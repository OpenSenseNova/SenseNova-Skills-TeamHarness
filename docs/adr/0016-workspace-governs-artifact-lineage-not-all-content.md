# Workspace governs Artifact lineage, not all content

The Workspace is authoritative for an Artifact's identity, version, access, provenance, and relationship to Runs, Agents, optional WorkItems, and validation, but Artifact content may reside either in Workspace-managed storage or at a verified external version such as a repository commit or document revision. The Workspace retains the submitted and validated historical facts if external content later disappears, and policy may require archival copies for results that must remain independently reviewable.
