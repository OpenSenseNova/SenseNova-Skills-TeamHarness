# Invitation targets verified email, not a bearer link

A Workspace Invitation targets one normalized verified email and may exist before its Human registers or installs a client. Possession of its link grants nothing: acceptance requires an authenticated Human who currently proves that exact verified email, then records the stable Human identity atomically with the new Membership; later email changes do not rewrite accepted history.

This enables pre-registration invitations without making forwarded or leaked links sufficient authority. Email verification and normalization stay behind the authentication seam, while the Workspace domain owns Invitation lifecycle, matching result, Membership creation, and audit.
