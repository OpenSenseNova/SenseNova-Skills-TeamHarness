# Conversation has no Owner or Administrator in the MVP

Conversation creator is immutable audit provenance, not a durable Owner or Administrator. Channel governance uses current active Human audience membership plus an audited Workspace Owner recovery path, while DM participants are fixed and a different participant set creates a new Conversation. Workspace Owner role alone does not grant content access, but an Owner may explicitly add their own Membership to a Channel and then receives full-history access. The MVP therefore has no Conversation ownership, administrator membership, transfer, or orphan lifecycle.

This keeps one source of governance authority and avoids parallel permission states whose conflicts would require precedence rules. The trade-off is that the MVP cannot grant a Human administration rights for only one Conversation; that capability requires a future explicit role model rather than overloading creator identity.
